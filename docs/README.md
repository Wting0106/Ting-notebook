## About Me

My doctoral research focuses on 3D reconstruction and 3D scene understanding, with an emphasis on instance segmentation, semantic querying, and representation optimization for 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS). Moving forward, I plan to explore 4D visual reconstruction and temporal scene understanding, particularly their applications in autonomous driving simulation. The content of this blog is adapted from selected parts of my personal notes and will be updated from time to time.
